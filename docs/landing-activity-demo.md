# Landing activity demo

Public, fictional sample: "Uma aventura no jardim". No patient data or private
professional materials are used. This is an illustrative activity, not a clinical
assessment. The three steps are observation, initial sound selection, and story
sequencing. The existing Embla carousel handles slide navigation.

## Artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original generated PNGs are
copied into the project without modification:

- `public/landing/aventura-jardim.png`: garden scene, 1536 x 1024.
- `public/landing/sequencia-jardim.png`: three equal story panels, 2172 x 724.
  CSS displays the individual panels without creating altered image files.

## Generation prompts

Garden: A polished preschool picture-book illustration, landscape 3:2. Two
children in a sunny garden: a brown-skinned curly-haired girl in yellow overalls
watering a seedling with a red watering can, and a boy in a turquoise shirt
holding a plant pot. Recognizable green frog by a pond, blue shoes, yellow bucket,
red-and-white ball, yellow sun, trees and flowers. Contemporary tactile gouache,
subtle paper texture, vibrant balanced green/yellow/blue/coral colors. Visually
separated objects, no typography, logo, UI, border or frame.

Sequence: One wide 3:1 picture-book sprite with exactly three equal square panels,
no gaps, frames, text, numbers or arrows. Consistent girl in yellow overalls and
hair bow, terracotta pot and red watering can. Left: planting a seed in soil.
Middle: watering a two-leaf sprout. Right: admiring a blooming pink flower.
Simple sunny garden backgrounds and premium textured gouache style. Each action
and the whole pot clearly visible in its panel.
